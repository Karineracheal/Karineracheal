## What is Tago?

Tago is a live mobile social platform where people watch together, talk in real time, and build communities around shared interests. It combines video, direct messaging, group chat, voice and video calls, and community features in a single native mobile experience.

Built from the ground up by a small team — designed, shipped, and iterated in public.

---

## Features

- 📺 **Live Video** — watch and stream in real time with your community
- 💬 **Direct Messaging** — one-on-one and group chat
- 📞 **Voice & Video Calls** — built-in calling across the platform
- 👥 **Communities** — create and join spaces around any interest
- 🛡️ **Content Moderation** — AI-assisted moderation to keep communities safe at scale
- 🔔 **Real-time Notifications** — stay connected without missing a moment
