## What is Aplique?

Job searching is broken. You spend hours writing cover letters, filling in the same forms, and losing track of where you applied. Aplique fixes that.

Aplique is an AI job platform that takes the manual labour out of job hunting — it applies for roles on your behalf and gives you a live dashboard tracking every application, its status, and what's next.

---

## What it does

- 🤖 **AI Applications** — Aplique applies for jobs for you, tailoring each application to the role
- 📊 **Application Tracking** — one dashboard showing every application, its status, and timeline
- 🔍 **Job Matching** — surfaces relevant roles based on your profile, skills, and preferences
- 📝 **Auto-filled Forms** — no more filling in the same information on every job board
- 📬 **Status Updates** — know when applications are viewed, progressed, or closed

---

## The Problem We're Solving

The average job seeker applies to 100+ roles before landing an offer. Each application takes 20–40 minutes manually. Aplique reduces that to seconds — and makes sure nothing falls through the cracks.
