## What is Moria.ai?

Moria.ai turns written stories into visual film. Upload a book chapter or film script, choose your style, and Moria generates a sequence of scenes — stitched together into a continuous short video with visual and narrative continuity.

It's a generative video pipeline designed for non-technical users: no prompt engineering, no model tuning — just a story in, a film out.

---

## How it works

1. **Upload** — paste or upload a book excerpt or film script
2. **Style Selection** — choose from a curated set of visual styles (cinematic, animated, documentary, etc.)
3. **Scene Generation** — Moria breaks the script into scenes and generates each one
4. **Review & Refine** — course-correct AI output scene by scene without touching the underlying model
5. **Connect & Export** — stitch scenes into a continuous sequence and export your film

---

## The Hard Problems We Solved

**Scene continuity** — generating individual scenes is easy. Making them feel like one film is hard. Moria preserves visual and narrative continuity as scenes connect and extend.

**AI transparency** — generative video is slow, variable, and imperfect. Rather than hiding that behind a spinner, Moria surfaces it clearly so users stay in control of the output.

**Non-technical UX** — a multi-stage generative AI pipeline is complex. Moria's interface makes it a simple, scannable sequence any user can follow.
